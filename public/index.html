Link your WhatsApp number to the bot.

  1. Open WhatsApp on the phone with this number
  2. Settings > Linked devices > Link a device
  3. Tap "Link with phone number instead"
  4. Enter the code above

Linking gives the bot access to read and send messages on that WhatsApp account. Only link a number you own, and unlink any time here or from WhatsApp > Linked devices.
